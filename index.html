Our Range

AC Types for Every Need

From compact home cooling to heavy-duty commercial systems — find the right air conditioner type for your space.

Split AC

Wall-mounted indoor units with outdoor compressor — quiet, efficient home & office cooling.

Window AC

All-in-one unit fitted in a window or wall opening — simple, budget-friendly cooling for single rooms.

Cassette AC

Ceiling-mounted 4-way airflow units — ideal for offices, showrooms and open spaces.

Tower AC

Floor-standing units with high airflow — perfect for halls, lobbies and large rooms.

Ductable AC

Concealed ducted systems for whole-building cooling — hospitals, hotels and offices.

VRF AC

Variable Refrigerant Flow systems for multi-zone commercial cooling — efficient & scalable.

Portable AC

Movable floor unit with a window hose — flexible cooling without installation.

Floor / Ceiling AC

Convertible units that mount under the ceiling or stand on the floor — wide, even airflow for long rooms.

Multi-Split AC

One outdoor unit connected to several indoor units — cool multiple rooms with one compressor.

Packaged AC

Heavy-duty single-cabinet systems for shops, restaurants, halls and small factories.
